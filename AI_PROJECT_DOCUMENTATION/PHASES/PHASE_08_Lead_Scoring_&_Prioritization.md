# Phase 08 — Lead Scoring & Prioritization

## Objective

Phase 08 is the intelligence ranking layer of the Lead Intelligence Platform.

The objective is **not** to discover additional information, crawl new websites, or call external APIs. Instead, this phase consumes the structured outputs generated by Phases 01–07 and produces a transparent, explainable, configurable, and deterministic lead score.

The final score enables sales teams to immediately understand:

* Which companies should be contacted first.
* Why they received their score.
* Which business opportunities exist.
* How confident the platform is in the recommendation.
* Which sales strategy should be used.

This phase must be suitable for processing **hundreds of thousands of companies** in batch mode with consistent results.

---

# 2. Weighted Scoring Engine

Every signal contributes to the final score.

Default weights:

| Category               | Weight |
| ---------------------- | -----: |
| Website Intelligence   |     15 |
| Contact Intelligence   |     15 |
| Decision Makers        |     20 |
| Business Intelligence  |     15 |
| Marketing Intelligence |     15 |
| AI Opportunities       |     15 |
| Confidence Adjustment  |      5 |

Weights must be configurable.

---

# 3. Grade Calculator

Convert score into a grade.

Example:

| Score  | Grade |
| ------ | ----- |
| 90–100 | A+    |
| 85–89  | A     |
| 80–84  | A−    |
| 75–79  | B+    |
| 70–74  | B     |
| 65–69  | C+    |
| 60–64  | C     |
| 50–59  | D     |
| <50    | F     |

# 4. Priority Engine

Assign sales priority.

Possible values:

* HOT
* HIGH
* MEDIUM
* LOW
* COLD

Priority should consider both score and confidence.

---

# 5. Confidence Engine

Generate an overall confidence score using:

* Data completeness
* Number of verified signals
* Agreement between analyzers
* Decision-maker verification
* Missing critical information

Range:

0.00–1.00

---

# 6. Explanation Engine

Every score must be explainable.

Produce:

## Positive Contributors

Examples:

* Executive identified
* Strong trust signals
* Active hiring
* Multiple service locations
* Mature marketing stack

---

## Negative Contributors

Examples:

* Slow website
* Poor SEO
* Missing decision makers
* Weak conversion funnel
* Missing security headers

---

## Reason Codes

Machine-readable identifiers, for example:

* EXEC_FOUND
* NO_CONTACT_FORM
* LOW_SEO_SCORE
* STRONG_SOCIAL
* ACTIVE_HIRING
* LOW_ACCESSIBILITY
* VERIFIED_PHONE
* VERIFIED_EMAIL

These codes support analytics and future CRM automation.
